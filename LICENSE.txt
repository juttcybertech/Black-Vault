================================================================================

6.1  ZERO-KNOWLEDGE ARCHITECTURE
     Black Vault is designed with a zero-knowledge security architecture. 
     The Licensor does NOT have access to:
     (a) Your encryption keys or passwords;
     (b) The contents of Your encrypted Vaults;
     (c) Any Personal Data stored within the Software.

6.2  ENCRYPTION STANDARDS
     The Software utilizes military-grade AES-256 (Advanced Encryption 
     Standard with 256-bit key length) encryption to protect Your data. 
     This is the same standard used by government agencies and financial 
     institutions worldwide.

6.3  DATA RESPONSIBILITY
     You are solely responsible for:
     (a) Maintaining the confidentiality of Your encryption passwords;
     (b) Creating and securely storing backup copies of Your encryption keys;
     (c) Any data loss resulting from forgotten passwords or lost keys.

6.4  DATA RECOVERY DISCLAIMER
     Due to the zero-knowledge architecture, the Licensor CANNOT recover 
     Your data if You lose or forget Your encryption password. The Licensor 
     shall not be held liable for any data loss resulting from lost or 
     forgotten passwords.

6.5  LOCAL PROCESSING
     All encryption and decryption operations are performed locally on 
     Your Device. No unencrypted data is transmitted to the Licensor's 
     servers at any time.

6.6  DATA COLLECTION
     The Software may collect the following non-personal information for 
     improvement purposes:
     (a) Application crash reports and error logs (anonymized);
     (b) Software version and operating system information;
     (c) General usage statistics (feature usage frequency).

     No personal files, passwords, or encryption keys are ever collected 
     or transmitted.
